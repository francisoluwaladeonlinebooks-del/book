# Independent clinical review packet

## Status: not yet reviewed

As of 19 November 2026, no licensed clinician has reviewed this manuscript. The signed review has not been arranged or completed, and this book must not be described as "medically reviewed", "clinically reviewed" or "peer reviewed" until a qualified person signs the form at the end of this packet.

What has been done: an evidence audit of the uploaded proof (`evidence-audit.md`), a rewrite that removes the unsupported statistics, and a reference list that was checked against public sources. That editorial work is not a substitute for clinical review.

Who arranges it: the author. A coding agent cannot register, credential or pay a reviewer, and inventing a reviewer's name or approval would be dishonest.

## Reviewers to approach

Ask at least two of the following, with current licences that can be checked:

1. A consultant obstetrician and gynaecologist, preferably with an interest in PCOS, endometriosis or menopause.
2. An endocrinologist or a physician with a specialist interest in thyroid disease and autoimmune conditions.
3. A registered dietitian for chapters 7, 10 and 11 and Appendix B.
4. A clinical psychologist or psychiatrist for chapter 9 and the crisis guidance.
5. A public-health physician for chapter 16.

In Nigeria, verify registration with the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria and the relevant professional council. Elsewhere, use the national or state regulator. A reviewer should disclose conflicts of interest, including any commercial relationship with supplement, app or hormone products.

## Material to send

- `Bio-Harmonization-KDP-interior.pdf` (the proposed interior).
- `editorial/evidence-audit.md` (what was removed and why).
- `manuscript/references.md` (all cited sources, grouped by chapter).

Ask the reviewer to focus on accuracy, omissions that could lead to harm, wording that implies a treatment effect, and the red-flag advice. Ask for tracked comments against page numbers in the interior PDF.

## Reviewer checklist

For each item, mark Agree / Change required / Not qualified to comment, and give a page number.

- [ ] Medical disclaimer is prominent and adequate for an educational book.
- [ ] No statement implies that a habit prevents, cures or reverses a disease.
- [ ] PCOS section matches current guideline care (Chapter 8).
- [ ] Menopause and hormone-therapy section is balanced and does not promote compounded products (Chapter 8).
- [ ] Stress chapter does not validate "adrenal fatigue" as a diagnosis (Chapter 3).
- [ ] Autoimmune chapter does not promote elimination diets or supplement protocols as treatment (Chapter 11).
- [ ] Vitamin D and laboratory-interval wording is consistent with current guidance (Appendix B).
- [ ] Fasting cautions cover pregnancy, breastfeeding, diabetes, medication and eating disorders (Chapter 10).
- [ ] Urgent symptoms and crisis guidance are clear and locally appropriate (Chapters 1, 3, 9, 11, 12).
- [ ] Case stories are clearly composites and cannot be read as clinical evidence.
- [ ] Statistics match the cited sources and are described correctly (models vs measurements).
- [ ] Any remaining claim that needs a citation or deletion is listed below.
