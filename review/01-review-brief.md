# Review brief

This document combines the revised book with the material needed to judge it. It is one PDF so that an editor or clinician can follow a claim from the page where it appears, through the evidence note, to the reference at the back.

The book is an educational guide to organising women's health information. It is written by a technology professional with no clinical qualification. It makes no treatment claims. The author asks reviewers to check whether that boundary holds on every page.

## What is inside

The first section is the complete manuscript, including front matter, five parts, sixteen chapters, an epilogue and four appendices. Page numbers in the contents refer to the page numbers printed in this PDF.

The second section is the evidence audit. It lists the claims in the uploaded proof that were removed, reframed or verified, and gives the reason for each decision.

The third section is the master edit report. It records the changes chapter by chapter and the prose pass applied to the manuscript.

The fourth section is the clinical review packet. It names the reviewers to approach, gives a checklist, and provides a claim register and a blank sign-off form.

The fifth section is the submission checklist and suggested KDP metadata.

The final section is a query log for your comments.

## What needs checking

Clinical reviewers should focus on accuracy, wording that could be read as a treatment claim, and advice that might delay care. Editors should focus on clarity, consistency of voice, the distinction between population data and one person's experience, and whether the composite stories are unmistakable.

Please check the medical disclaimer, the PCOS and menopause passages, the stress and autoimmune chapters, the fasting cautions, Appendix B and the urgent-symptom advice. These are the places where an imprecise sentence could cause harm.

## How to respond

Use the query log at the end. Give the printed page number, the section and a short statement of the problem. Add a suggested replacement where you have one. If you are a clinician, complete the sign-off form in the clinical review packet only if you agree with the wording and are willing to be named.

When commenting on a statistic, please state the source you would accept in its place. When commenting on tone, quote the sentence and propose an alternative. That makes the next revision quicker.

## Status and disclosure

The manuscript was rebuilt from a print proof that contained unattributed statistics, an unverifiable institutional identity and a proofing artefact. Those items were removed. The revision was produced with AI assistance that generated and edited text. The author checked the retained claims against the sources listed in the book and accepts responsibility for the final wording.

No licensed clinician has reviewed this manuscript. The book must not be described as medically reviewed until the sign-off form is completed.
