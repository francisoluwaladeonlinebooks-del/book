# Appendix D: Glossary

Androgen: A group of hormones that includes testosterone. Higher-than-expected levels are one feature of PCOS.

Autoimmune disease: A condition in which the immune system attacks the body's own tissue.

Basal body temperature: Body temperature taken at rest, usually on waking. It can confirm that ovulation has happened; it is a weak predictor on its own.

Bio-Harmonization: The title concept of this book. A way of organising observations about sleep, cycle, mood and energy. It is not a diagnosis or a treatment.

BMI: Body mass index, a weight-for-height measure used in research and some clinical decisions. It says little about muscle or fitness in one person.

Circadian rhythm: The roughly 24-hour cycle that organises sleep, alertness and other body processes.

Coeliac disease: An autoimmune condition triggered by gluten in genetically susceptible people. It is distinct from non-coeliac gluten sensitivity.

Endocrine-disrupting chemical: A substance that can interfere with hormone signalling. Exposure and effect vary by compound and dose.

Endometriosis: A condition in which tissue similar to the uterine lining grows outside the uterus. It can cause pain and infertility. WHO estimates that it affects about 10% of reproductive-age women.

HbA1c: A blood test reflecting average glucose over about three months.

HPA axis: The hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal system, which coordinates the stress response.

HPO axis: The hypothalamic-pituitary-ovarian system, which coordinates reproductive hormone signalling.

Inflammation: A response to injury or threat. It can be helpful in the short term and harmful when persistent.

LH: Luteinising hormone. A surge precedes ovulation; home tests detect it in urine.

Menopause: The point after 12 consecutive months without a menstrual period, in the absence of another cause.

Night sweats: Episodes of sweating during sleep, common in perimenopause and menopause but also associated with other conditions.

Oligo-ovulation: Infrequent or irregular ovulation, one criterion used in PCOS assessment.

Perimenopause: The transition in the years before menopause, when cycles and symptoms often change.

Polyvagal theory: A framework for understanding autonomic responses to safety and threat. Some of its claims remain debated.

PCOS: Polycystic ovary syndrome, a common hormonal and metabolic condition with variable symptoms.

Reference interval: The range of results seen in a defined reference population, usually covering the middle 95%. It is not the same as a healthy target for an individual.

TSH: Thyroid-stimulating hormone, usually the first test when thyroid function is assessed.

Vasomotor symptoms: Hot flushes and night sweats associated with changes in oestrogen.

Vitamin D (25-OHD): The form measured in blood to assess vitamin D status. Guidelines disagree about target levels, and routine testing is not advised for healthy adults.
