Do not start, stop or change medication, hormone therapy, supplements, fasting, or any treatment plan because of something you read here. Bring your questions to a qualified healthcare professional who knows your history. A period of illness, pregnancy, breastfeeding, an eating disorder, kidney disease, diabetes, or treatment with prescription medicines changes the advice in this book.

Call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department for chest pain, difficulty breathing, fainting, sudden severe headache, weakness on one side of the body, heavy bleeding, severe abdominal or pelvic pain, thoughts of self-harm, or any symptom that feels acutely dangerous.

Nothing in this book promises recovery, reversal, cure or a particular outcome. The word "protocol" is used here to mean a repeatable way of organising information and habits. It does not describe a clinically tested treatment.
