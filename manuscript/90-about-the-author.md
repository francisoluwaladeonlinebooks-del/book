# About the author

Olabode Johnson Ilesanmi is a Nigerian technology professional with a BSc in Computer Science and professional experience in information technology, data analysis and systems management. He has worked as an IT manager and has training in data analysis and visualisation, including data analytics study through Cisco and the 3MTT programme.

His interest in research, data-driven decision-making and the relationship between information and human wellbeing shapes this book. *Bio-Harmonization: The Bureau Protocol* applies a systems perspective to women's health education: how to organise health information, notice patterns, evaluate evidence and prepare better questions for healthcare professionals.

Ilesanmi is not a clinician. The framework in this book is a tool for organising information and supporting conversations. Clinical decisions belong with qualified healthcare professionals.
